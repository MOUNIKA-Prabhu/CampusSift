Evaluation Methodology

  • Ground Truth Dataset: Manually labeled resume–JD pairs with expert relevance tags.
  • Relevance Threshold: Candidates with overall match score ≥ 50% classified as relevant.
  • Top-1 Accuracy: Checks if ground-truth top candidate is ranked #1 by the model.
  • Top-3 Accuracy: Checks if top ground-truth candidate is included in top-3 predictions.
